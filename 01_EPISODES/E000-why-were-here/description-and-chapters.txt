We got married a few weeks ago, started our second year of business school at Stanford, and apparently thought now was a great time to start a podcast. About AI. A thing we’re very much still figuring out ourselves.

This first one is mostly us: who we are, why we’re doing this, and why we think everyone should be learning AI (yes, even the haters). We also talk about running a youth sports nonprofit together, the 15-ish puppies we’ve fostered, and the time Josh had AI make family holiday photos of our future kids on our honeymoon.

What should we try next? Drop it in the comments or email hi@outofourdepth.com. We read everything (…there’s not much yet).
