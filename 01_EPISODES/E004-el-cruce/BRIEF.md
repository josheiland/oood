## One-sentence promise
Turn a vague request into a useful plan by supplying context, defining the output, checking the first attempt and giving specific feedback.

## Why now

## Core question
What does AI need from us before its output becomes useful?

## Working thesis
The useful skill is directing and checking the work, including deciding when the result is good enough. This is an editorial recommendation, not an approved title or a claim that an AI training plan is medically suitable.

## Audience starting point

5. Check flawed outputs and refine them.
6. Show the working website and phone result.
