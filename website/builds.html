Built with Claude

This website

You are looking at it. We described what we wanted and Claude wrote every line of code

How we built it

  1. We said what we wanted in plain words and pasted screenshots of sites we love
  2. Claude built a few versions. We picked one, the same way you would pick a paint color
  3. Then we nitpicked for hours. "No lines." "Funnier." "Make us float." That part is where it starts to feel like yours

Your turn. Copy this into any AI app

Build me a simple one-page website for [what it is for]. Here are two sites I like the look of: [links or screenshots]. Show me three different designs first so I can pick one, then build the one I choose.

Mary's voice guide

A cheat sheet on how Mary writes, so drafts sound like her instead of a robot. It knows she says "thank you so big" and never uses a long dash

How we built it

  1. We skipped describing her style and gave AI the real thing: emails and texts she already sent
  2. AI pulled out the patterns, like her favorite phrases, how she signs off and what she never says
  3. Every time a draft sounds off, she corrects it and the guide gets the fix. It keeps getting more her

Your turn. Copy this into any AI app

Interview me one question at a time about how I write: who I usually write to, words I use a lot, and things I'd never say. After eight questions, write a short voice guide I can paste into my custom instructions.

An AI coach for a trail race

We signed up for El Cruce and set up a coach plus a logistics helper. Then we had to coach the coach

How we built it

  1. We made one project so everything about the race lives in the same place
  2. We told it exactly what we want from a coach before asking for a single workout
  3. When it got things wrong, we said so. The plan got better every time we pushed back

Your turn. Copy this into any AI app

You're my coach for [goal]. Start by asking me five questions about where I am now and what gets in my way. Then give me a plan for this week. Every time I come back, ask how it went before you give advice. Be honest, not a cheerleader.

The AI from 0 to 1 study guide

50 words, 10 debates and 5 ways to start. We wrote the list. AI did the layout, the PDF and the fact-checking legwork

How we built it

  1. We started with the words we kept having to explain to each other on the show
  2. AI searched our episode captions to find the exact minute we explain each word
  3. We asked for five different designs and picked the one that gets deeper as you scroll

Your turn. Copy this into any AI app

I want to make a beginner's guide to [topic I know well]. Interview me to find the 20 words a beginner needs, then write each one in plain English with a "think of it like" comparison. Show me two layout ideas before you build it.
