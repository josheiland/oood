Our wedding tracker

Download the tracker

See the prompt

Attach the tracker first

We're planning our wedding and I've attached a starter tracker. Before you change anything, interview us one question at a time: the date and place, a rough guest count and budget, which events we're hosting (a welcome party, a rehearsal dinner, a breakfast the day after), and what we care about and what we'll skip. Ask about anything first-timers usually forget. Then rewrite the tracker for us: cut what we're not doing, add what's missing, and give every task a category, a due date, an owner, a status and a priority

Our wedding weekend website

One page for the schedule, maps, FAQs and guest photos. We showed Claude our original site so the new one felt like us

See the prompt

Build a one-page website for guests at our wedding weekend in [place]. Include the schedule with add-to-calendar buttons and map links, shuttle times, a short FAQ (dress code, parking, kids), a few of our favorite local spots and a button to upload photos to a shared folder. Match the colors and fonts of our wedding site: [link]. Make it easy to use on a phone, and write it the way we talk: [a few lines about us]
