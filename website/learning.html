The bite-size pieces AI reads and writes in. One token is about three quarters of a word, which is why limits and prices get counted in tokens instead of words.

Context window

How much the AI can hold in its head at once, including your messages, its replies and any files you drop in. Really long chats can push early details out, which is why they get forgetful.

A model that takes a beat to work through a problem before it answers. Slower, but a lot better at math, planning and anything with a few moving parts.

3 FTWord to know

Agent

AI you hand a goal instead of a question. It picks the steps, uses tools like search or your calendar, and keeps going until it's done or stuck.

Try thisGive an agent a small errand, like finding three dinner spots with a table for two on Friday. Watch what it does.

3 FTWord to know

Automation

Workflow

A fixed set of steps that runs the same way every time, like "when a receipt hits my inbox, save it to this folder." Unlike an agent, it doesn't make decisions along the way.

Try thisWrite down one thing you do every week in the exact same steps. That's an automation waiting to happen.

3 FTWord to know

Vibe coding

Building an app by describing what you want and letting AI write all the code. Andrej Karpathy coined it in February 2025, and Collins made it Word of the Year.

Try thisAsk an AI to build a one-page website for your dog. Don't touch a single line of code.
