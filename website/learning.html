The learning list

Topics we've been thinking about

The AI words and ideas that keep coming up, explained the way we'd explain them to each other. Upvote anything you want a whole episode on.

  1. Generative AI

    Not on the show yet

    AI that makes new things, like writing, images, music or video.

    ThinkThe difference between a spam filter and a ghostwriter.

  2. Prompt

    Ep 004 at 16:23

    Whatever you type or say to the AI. The best ones read like a text to a smart friend who knows nothing about your life.

    ThinkOrdering "food, please" vs. "the salmon, sauce on the side."

  3. LLM

    Large language model

    Ep 002 at 06:20

    The engine inside ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini. It read a huge chunk of the internet and learned to guess the next word.

    ThinkAutocomplete that read the whole library.

  4. Model vs. app

    Who's who

    Ep 002 at 08:49

    OpenAI is the company. GPT-6 Astra is one of its models. ChatGPT is the app you open.

    ThinkToyota, the engine and the Prius.

  5. Hallucination

    Ep 003 at 22:09

    When AI makes something up with a straight face, like a quote nobody said or a study that doesn't exist.

    ThinkThe friend who'd rather invent an answer than say "I don't know."

  6. Multimodal

    Ep 002 at 08:29

    AI that understands more than text. Send it a photo or a voice memo.

    ThinkSnap a photo of your fridge and ask what's for dinner.

  7. Memory

    Not on the show yet

    The app remembering things about you between chats, like your job or that you're vegetarian. You can edit it in settings.

    ThinkA barista who knows your usual.

  8. Custom instructions

    Ep 004 at 09:09

    Notes the AI reads before every chat, like "keep it short" or "I run a nonprofit."

    ThinkThe note on the fridge for the babysitter.

  9. Projects

    Ep 001 at 01:43

    A folder for one ongoing thing, with its own chats, files and instructions, so you never re-explain the backstory.

    ThinkGiving your AI its own desk.

  10. Model sizes

    Not on the show yet

    Most AI companies sell a few sizes. Bigger is smarter, smaller is faster and cheaper. OpenAI's are Astra, Sol and Luna.

    ThinkCoffee sizes. Not every question needs a venti.

  11. Training

    Ep 002 at 06:20

    How a model learns. It reads a mountain of text over and over until the patterns stick.

    ThinkYears of reading before the first day on the job.

  12. Knowledge cutoff

    Not on the show yet

    The date a model's built-in knowledge stops. Ask about anything newer and it's guessing, unless it can search the web.

    ThinkA smart friend back from six months off the grid.

  13. AI slop

    Ep 001 at 15:42

    The bland, over-polished writing AI churns out when nobody edits it. "I hope this finds you well," forever.

    ThinkElevator music, but for words.

  14. Sycophancy

    Ep 004 at 23:43

    When AI tells you what you want to hear, like calling your so-so business plan brilliant.

    ThinkA yes-man with a big vocabulary.

  15. Deepfake

    Not on the show yet

    Fake but real-looking video, audio or photos of a real person. Voice clones make scam calls sound like family.

    ThinkPhotoshop, if Photoshop could call your grandma.

  16. Token

    Ep 002 at 13:26

    The bite-size chunks AI reads and writes in, about three quarters of a word each. Prices and limits are counted in them.

    ThinkGas in the car. Every word in and out burns a little.

  17. Context window

    Not on the show yet

    How much the AI can hold in its head at once, including your messages, its replies and your files. In long chats, the early stuff starts to slip.

    ThinkA whiteboard. Fill it up and something gets erased.

  18. Reasoning model

    Thinking mode

    Ep 004 at 13:36

    A model that thinks through a problem before answering. Slower, but much better at math and anything with moving parts.

    ThinkShowing your work instead of blurting out the first number.

  19. Deep research

    Not on the show yet

    Ask a big question, go make coffee, come back to a report with sources. The AI runs dozens of searches for you.

    ThinkAn intern who does the library run for you.

  20. Vibe coding

    Ep 004 at 23:52

    Building an app by describing it in plain English and letting AI write all the code.

    ThinkTelling a builder what house you want and never looking at the blueprints.

  21. Agent

    All of ep 003

    AI you give a goal instead of a question. It picks the steps, uses tools like search or your calendar, and keeps going until it's done.

    ThinkAsking for directions vs. handing someone your car keys.

  22. Skills

    Ep 001 at 08:12

    Saved instructions for specific jobs, like your brand rules or how you write a grant. The AI grabs the right one when it needs it.

    ThinkA binder of how-tos a new hire actually reads.

  23. Computer use

    Not on the show yet

    AI using a computer the way you do, looking at the screen, clicking and typing.

    ThinkHanding someone your laptop and watching over their shoulder.

  24. MCP

    Model Context Protocol

    Ep 001 at 05:59

    A standard plug that connects AI to your other apps, like Gmail or Google Drive, so it can get things done there.

    ThinkUSB for AI. One plug shape, lots of devices.

  25. Prompt injection

    Not on the show yet

    Sneaky instructions hidden in a webpage or email that trick AI into doing something you didn't ask for.

    ThinkA note slipped into the to-do pile: "also, wire $500 to this account."

  26. Open vs. closed models

    Ep 002 at 06:20

    Open models, like DeepSeek and Qwen, can be downloaded and run by anyone. Closed ones, like the models in ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini, only live behind the company's app.

    ThinkA recipe you can cook at home vs. a dish you can only order at the restaurant.

  27. Benchmark

    Not on the show yet

    A standardized test for AI. Labs love bragging about scores, and the tests keep getting harder because models keep acing them.

    ThinkThe SAT, for robots.

  28. GPU

    Graphics processing unit

    Not on the show yet

    The chips that do AI's heavy math. Nvidia makes most of them, and everyone wants more.

    ThinkGaming graphics cards that got drafted into a much bigger job.

  29. Data center

    Not on the show yet

    Warehouses full of those chips, running around the clock. Their power and water use is a big part of the AI debate.

    ThinkThe kitchen behind every AI answer.

  30. RAG

    Retrieval-augmented generation

    Not on the show yet

    AI that checks your documents before answering, so it can cite sources instead of going from memory.

    ThinkAn open-book test instead of a closed-book one.

  31. Fine-tuning

    Not on the show yet

    Extra training on a focused set of examples so a model gets great at one job, like writing in your company's voice.

    ThinkA residency after med school.

  32. Scaling laws

    Not on the show yet

    The pattern that models get smarter as they get bigger and train on more data with more chips. Whether it keeps holding is the trillion-dollar question.

    ThinkAdding horsepower to a car. Works great, until it doesn't.

  33. Alignment

    Not on the show yet

    Getting AI to do what people actually want, without cutting corners or causing harm. It matters more as models get smarter.

    ThinkRaising a kid who'll end up smarter than you.

  34. AGI

    Artificial general intelligence

    Not on the show yet

    AI that can do pretty much any thinking job a person can. Nobody agrees on the definition or when it arrives.

    ThinkA finish line everyone's racing toward, but each team drew it somewhere else.

  35. Superintelligence

    Not on the show yet

    AI that beats the best humans at nearly everything. The step after AGI.

    ThinkThe student who passes the teacher and keeps going.
