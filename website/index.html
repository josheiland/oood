About

Who are we

We're Mary and Josh, newlyweds at Stanford Business School, where we help lead AI@GSB, the dean's Applied AI Initiative. Before landing here, we both studied systems engineering at the University of Virginia and worked in consulting at Bain & Company

We started Out of Our Depth to learn AI out loud and make it fun, so nobody (including us) feels silly for asking what something means or how to use it
