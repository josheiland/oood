# OOOD release package

Read live project state, the finished episode's edit/QC/claim notes, [editorial direction](references/editorial-direction.md), and [brand guide](references/brand-guide.md). Fall back to [show context](references/show-context.md) only if the repository is unavailable. Never claim to have viewed or heard media that was not accessible.

Make the package invite people into Josh and Mary's dynamic. Prefer moments with a clear setup and payoff: an affectionate disagreement, a surprising real result, a useful confession, or one host changing the other's mind. Do not manufacture reaction shots, remove a caveat that changes meaning, or attribute editorial copy as a quote.

## Outputs

Match scope to the request. A full release package should include:

- A few distinct title/thumbnail directions grounded in the finished story. Thumbnail copy is at most three words; use the supplied visual kit and check at 210px wide. No invented time savings or autonomous success claims.
- Show notes beginning with one action to try, a brief episode description, exact final-export chapters, definitions where helpful, and dated sources for factual claims. Mark host opinions as opinions.
- For shared YouTube/Spotify descriptions, start chapters at 00:00, use at least three chronological plain-text entries, and verify at least 30 seconds between starts. OOOD also keeps the final chapter at least 30 seconds long. Merge short sections at their real topic boundaries; don't shift timestamps away from the content merely to pass. Josh requested this default on 2026-09-30 after a YouTube-only check missed Spotify's spacing. [Spotify chapter guidance](https://support.spotify.com/us/creators/article/episode-chapters/) was checked that day; recheck platform rules when preparing later releases.
- A short ranked clip list with source and final-export time ranges, context needed, payoff, caption hook, and edit notes. If asked to make clips, render actual files, not just the list. Reframe from the actual picture; preserve both hosts where the reaction is the point.
- Corrected captions aligned to each clip, burned into Shorts/Reels, with downloadable SRT/VTT. Check names, overlap, safe areas, timing, and contrast. Avoid word-by-word animation that fights a punchline unless specifically requested.
- Platform drafts: LinkedIn hook plus up to four short paragraphs; Instagram up to three lines and a closing question. Apply kit rules and current account handles from context. Confirm publication before writing “out now” as fact.
- A package inventory with file paths, dimensions, and status, plus any outstanding approval/source/rights issue that actually blocks release.

Derive clips from moments that exist; don't optimize every moment into a clickbait claim. Keep one clear reason to watch the full episode. No mandatory content calendar or forced quota of clips. Don't assume the kit's sample platforms are active distribution accounts.

Complete the requested assets before seeking any necessary final approval. Generating a package is not permission to post, schedule, email, or alter accounts. When publication is explicitly authorized, verify the resulting live state and record the actual URL/date, otherwise keep drafts local.
