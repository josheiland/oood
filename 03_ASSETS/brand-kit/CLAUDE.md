# Project notes

## Writing rules (always apply)
- No AI slop. Follow the humanizer skill: no em dashes, no rule-of-three padding, no "not just X but Y", no promotional adjectives (vibrant, robust, seamless, leverage, unlock, transformative), no emoji, straight quotes only.
- Clear and concise. Short sentences, one idea each. Cut words that do not change the meaning.
- Assume no tech background. Translate any jargon the first time it appears, then keep using the word.
- Empowering, never intimidating. The listener or reader is never made to feel behind. We are the ones who look silly.
- Make learning AI fun. Humor about ourselves, and every piece ends with something the person can actually try.
