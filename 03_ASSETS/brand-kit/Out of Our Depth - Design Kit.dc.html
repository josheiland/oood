12

Voice

One rule above the others: a listener with no tech background should finish every sentence knowing what we meant and feeling like they could try it. If a line sounds like a consultant wrote it, rewrite it.

Sounds like us
  • Say it the way you would say it to a friend who has never opened an AI tool.
  • Translate the jargon the first time, then keep using the word so it sticks.
  • Short sentences. One idea each. Read it out loud before you post it.
  • End with something the listener can try today.
  • We are the ones who look silly, never the listener.
Doesn't
  • "Unlock the power of AI." No thought-leader voice, ever.
  • Jargon with no translation, or numbers we cannot source.
  • Anything that makes a listener feel behind.
  • Em dashes, emoji, and words like leverage, robust, or seamless.
Title formulas
  • We tried [thing] so you don't have to
  • [Number] things nobody explains about [topic]
  • Is [tool] actually worth it?
  • One word + subtitle (thumbnail C)
