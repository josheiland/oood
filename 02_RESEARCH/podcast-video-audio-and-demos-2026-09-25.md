# Research note: Video, audio, and screen-share demonstrations

- **Date started:** 2026-09-25
- **Researcher:** Codex
- **Related episode(s):** E002 and overall show format
- **Status:** Evidence reviewed; editorial recommendation only, awaiting host choice

## Question

Do screen-share demonstrations improve engagement for a casual, relatable, entertaining AI podcast, or should episodes remain fully understandable through audio?

## Bottom line

Evidence supports offering both watching and listening. It does not establish that software demonstrations outperform host conversation. Recommend preserving a complete audio experience, filming host interaction, and selectively testing brief, verbally explained results. This is editorial inference, not a proven optimum or a host-approved decision.

## Definitions and comparability

Video availability, active watching, foreground playback, episode completion, and returning next month are different measures. Do not pool percentages across studies. None isolates screen shares from host camera footage. Spotify's retention comparison includes shows exceeding ten monthly consumption hours; video shows must have published video in the preceding 30 days. Random assignment and comparable hosts/topics are not established.

## Counterevidence and uncertainty

No controlled comparison answering the precise demo-versus-conversation question was identified in the reviewed sources. Topic, host popularity, production resources, distribution, and audience preferences can affect observed associations. Do not claim demonstrations increase retention by 30% or that research establishes an optimal demonstration length.

## Usable examples

- Show a short before/after result, describe the difference aloud, and keep host judgment and reactions central.
- Describe an AI failure through its task, mistake, and consequence.
- Offer detailed setup/navigation in an optional companion walkthrough.
- An occasional 30–60-second result reveal is a proposed experiment, not a research-backed duration.
- Evaluate retention around the segment, subsequent return, and audio listener comprehension across several episodes. Topic and segment position confound comparisons; small samples are directional.

## Script-ready language

Draft: People often move between listening and watching podcasts. We want the conversation to make sense either way, with occasional visuals that add something when you can look.
