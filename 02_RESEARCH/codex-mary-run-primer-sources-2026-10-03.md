### Project Vend recording cues — refreshed 2026-10-03

S25: [Phase two](https://www.anthropic.com/research/project-vend-2), same publishers, published 2025-12-18. Updated models, cost visibility, customer/order tracking and required research checks accompanied better performance; loss-making weeks became uncommon. Manipulation and false authority remained; humans intervened. The CEO shared model weaknesses and its contribution to profit was uncertain. These vendor-reported, evolving office trials do not isolate individual causes or establish unattended business reliability. This refresh supports live cues; no change to the delivered narration TXT.

- Ten independent invented steps at 95% each yield 0.95^10 = 0.5987369392, spoken as roughly 60%. The assumptions and absence of empirical significance are explicit in chapter 17.
- Chapter 16's invented attention calculation: 60 manual minutes minus 15 setup minus 20 review = 25 minutes saved, before any repair. Another 30 minutes of repair reverses that benefit. These are hypothetical, not measured RYC results.
- Venue A/B/C prices, the 1,500-dollar ceiling, 1,700-dollar reply and 1,300-dollar incomplete estimate in chapter 23 are invented teaching examples. No real venues or current prices are recommended.
- Sixteen developers, 246 tasks, 19% longer completion: S28. Randomized task-level AI access, familiar repositories and early-2025 tools are specified. No general claim about all AI productivity follows.
- METR's time horizon is defined by human expert task time at a success rate, not agent runtime. No model-specific numerical horizon is used.
- Project Vend is provider-reported and uses a small unusual office environment. Improvement across phases does not isolate a model, tools, or manager effect.
- Listening duration is word count divided by assumed words/minute. See the verification receipt for final count and hash. About two hours is an estimate; no rendered duration is claimed.
