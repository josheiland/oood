# Research

Store reusable research, claim-level source logs, product comparisons, primers, and episode-specific source packs here.

## Minimum source record

For every factual claim intended for publication, capture:

- the claim in the show's words;
- source title and publisher;
- URL;
- publication date and access date;
- relevant population, methodology, and sample size when applicable;
- limitations or comparability warnings;
- the episode(s) using it.

Use `templates/RESEARCH_NOTE_TEMPLATE.md` for new research notes.
