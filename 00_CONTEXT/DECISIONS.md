## 2026-09-21 — Record in 1080p, not 4K

**Decision:** Set the Logitech camera in both Logi Tune and OBS to 1920 × 1080. Do not use 4K unless a later need justifies it.

**Why:** 4K created accumulating camera delay and eventual audio/video desynchronization. Matching both camera-control software and OBS at 1080p eliminated nearly all delay in an approximately 15-minute test. The current format does not require digital zoom-ins.

**Status:** Current; validate during a full episode.
