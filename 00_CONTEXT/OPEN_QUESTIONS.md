# Open questions

Only unresolved decisions belong here. Move resolved items into `DECISIONS.md` and update `PROJECT_BRIEF.md` when needed.

## Launch

- Should the first three pieces release together, within a few days, or on the normal ongoing cadence?
- What is the exact launch date and the coordinated social push around it?
- Is the working twice-weekly cadence sustainable once editing and clip production are included?
- Which performance signals will trigger format, length, title, or cadence changes?

## Audience and editorial

- What is the sharpest one-sentence show description?
- How broad should the default audience be versus explicitly targeting leaders/operators?
- Which recurring segments, if any, should become part of the format?
- What standards determine whether a news item is consequential enough to cover?

## Production

- Which microphones were ultimately purchased and what are the final gain/filter settings?
- Does a full-length 1080p recording remain synchronized end to end?
- What is the canonical OBS scene collection and backup process?
- Who owns editing, thumbnails, captions, clips, quality control, and publishing?
- Where will raw recordings and final masters live outside Git?

## Brand and assets

- Which logo, cover, intro music, and visual variants are final?
- What are the canonical fonts, colors, safe areas, and platform-specific exports?
- Is a formal brand guide necessary now or after initial audience feedback?

## Distribution and newsletter

- What is the newsletter's specific job: episode recap, standalone analysis, source pack, community channel, or a combination?
- Which email platform and sender address will be used?
- Should a Facebook presence be added after launch, and what evidence would justify it?
- What is the repeatable workflow for turning a full episode into short clips and platform-native posts?

## Research and first episodes

- Which defensible statistics best establish that AI is important but nonprofit adoption/effectiveness lags?
- How should the show accurately compare skill/workflow capabilities across ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini?
- What are the current limits on connecting multiple Gmail/Google accounts, and when is Composio a credible workaround?
- Which claims from Run Your City experience can be shared publicly?
